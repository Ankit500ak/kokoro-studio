## How to Write Natural Text for Liam

### DO Use:
- Contractions: "I'm", "you're", "it's", "that's", "don't"
- Casual fillers: "so", "like", "you know", "honestly"
- Questions: "right?", "you know?"
- Short sentences mixed with longer flows
- Line breaks for natural pauses

### DON'T Use:
- Formal language
- Long, complex sentences without breaks
- Stiff, robotic phrasing
- Too many exclamation marks!!!

```

```

And honestly? It's kind of wild.

## Text Templates for Natural Speech

### Casual Intro:
```
Hey, what's up?

So I was literally just sitting here thinking about... you know that thing we talked about?

```
You know that moment when you're walking home and you see something that just stops you in your tracks?

That happened to me last week.

And honestly? I haven't been able to stop thinking about it since.

```
Look, I'm not gonna lie, I was skeptical at first.

But after trying it out, I totally get why everyone's been talking about this.

It's actually really good.
```

### Emotional:
```
Sometimes you just need to take a step back and breathe.

Like really breathe.

And remember that not everything has to be perfect.
```

## Pro Tips

1. **Read it aloud first** - If it sounds weird when you say it, it'll sound weird when Liam says it

2. **Use "..." for trailing thoughts** - Creates natural hesitation

3. **Break up long thoughts** - Multiple short paragraphs > one long block

4. **Add "So" at the start** - Very natural conversation starter

5. **Use rhetorical questions** - "You know?" "Right?" "Get what I mean?"

6. **Listen to the samples** - Compare different speeds and styles

7. **Preview before generating** - Always test a short sample first
