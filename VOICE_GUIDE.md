## Tone Presets

- **Natural** - Balanced, everyday tone
- **Warm** - Friendly, approachable
- **Deep** - Rich, authoritative
- **Smooth** - Polished, professional
- **Bright** - Upbeat, energetic
- **Gentle** - Soft, calming

## Speed Recommendations

- **Slow (0.85x)** - Dramatic emphasis
- **Normal (1.0x)** - Natural pace
- **Fast (1.1x)** - Energetic delivery
- **Shorts (1.05x)** - Optimized for shorts

## Tips for Human-Like Voices

1. **Use appropriate speed** - Not too fast, not too slow
2. **Match voice to content** - Light for casual, heavy for dramatic
3. **Use tone presets** - They adjust the voice character
4. **Preview before generating** - Test different voices
5. **Add natural breaks** - Use paragraphs for pauses
